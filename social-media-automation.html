FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does social media automation comply with platform terms of service?

Yes. We build exclusively on official, registered Meta Graph APIs and LinkedIn v2 APIs. We do not use web scraping or unofficial browser automation tools that violate terms or risk account suspension.

Can we review posts before they are published live?

Yes! By default, all generated posts are placed into an interactive Slack queue or lightweight dashboard. A single click approves the post for its scheduled time slot, or you can tweak the copy right inside Slack.

What content sources can trigger automatic posts?

We can ingest blog RSS feeds, Notion content tables, YouTube video uploads, GitHub releases, podcast episodes, or raw voice notes recorded on your phone.

How fast can this pipeline be set up?

A complete multi-channel social media automation pipeline (LinkedIn + Instagram + Facebook + Slack approval queue) typically ships in 5–7 days.
