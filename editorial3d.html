Editorial3D - PDF to 3D Flipbook Publishing

Editorial3D transforms flat, unread PDFs into photorealistic, page-turning 3D interactive flipbooks. Built for catalogs, annual reports, whitepapers, and sales brochures, it empowers businesses to capture high-intent leads with custom gating, measure page-level reading completion, and publish under their own branded custom domains with lifetime pricing and zero recurring subscriptions.

Publishing Pipeline

PDF to Interactive 3D Architecture

01 // INGEST
Vector PDF Processing

Handles up to 50MB files, decomposing pages into high-DPI vector assets and optimized cloud textures in seconds.

02 // 3D COMPILE
Photorealistic Flip

WebGL page-curl engine calculates real-time drag physics, ambient shadows, and spread flipping across all screens.

03 // GATE & DOMAIN
Lead Gate & White-Label

Embeds lead capture forms on targeted pages and serves publications under your brand domain with automated SSL.

04 // TELEMETRY
Depth Analytics & Webhooks

Monitors unique reader drop-offs per page and fires real-time webhooks to CRM pipelines on lead submissions.

What it Does

Full Product Capabilities Overview

Realistic 3D Viewer

A genuine 3D page-turning experience rather than a static iframe embed: readers drag corners, flip spreads, and zoom on high-DPI text.

Lead Capture Gates

Place customizable email capture gates right before high-value pages (e.g. before page 3 or after the executive summary) to collect warm leads.

Per-Publication Analytics

Analyze total views, unique readers, and exact reading depth percentages for each publication so you know which pages actually hold attention.

Custom Branded Domains

Publish directly at read.yourbrand.com instead of third-party domains. Point a simple CNAME and automated SSL certificates handle the rest.

Approval Workflows

Send interactive draft links for client or stakeholder sign-off prior to publishing. Reviewers experience the live 3D viewer rather than flat exports.

Webhooks & API

Fire webhook payloads on every newly captured lead or view threshold event, synchronizing reader data instantly into HubSpot, Slack, or n8n.

50MB High-Res Capacity

Built to ingest massive, image-heavy product catalogs, comprehensive corporate reports, and multi-page marketing collateral without stutter.

Embed & White-Label

Embed flipbooks seamlessly into WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or React applications with zero vendor branding, or distribute via direct URL.
