## Priority Issues

**[P0] Broken heading hierarchy from stacked `!important` overrides**
Why it matters: Every major section subhead is hardcoded to `font-size: 16px !important` inline, stacked on a separate global `h2 { font-size: 20px !important; font-weight: bold !important; }` rule (line 404). The Tailwind classes actually on those elements (`text-2xl md:text-3xl`) are dead code, never rendered. Headlines look flatter than intended, hurting scannability — the page's one job is guiding a skimming visitor to the CTA.
Fix: delete both `!important` blocks; let the existing Tailwind classes render as authored.
Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] Generic "AI-studio" visual identity (detector-confirmed)**
Why it matters: Dark + lime-neon + monospace + grid-overlay + glow-shadow is now the default look for this entire product category. Detector flagged the exact mechanical fingerprints (`overused-font` on Inter/Space Grotesk, `codex-grid-background`, `dark-glow`) independent of the LLM's "category-interchangeable" read — two independent methods landed on the same conclusion. Sameness is a conversion risk when every competitor looks identical.
Fix: pick one differentiating visual move — a less-saturated font pairing, drop or reinterpret the grid/glow motif, or lean into something literal to "TrailOps" (trail/outdoors-adjacent, or a genuinely different color story) instead of another shade of matrix-green.
Suggested command: `/impeccable bolder` or `/impeccable overdrive`

**[P1] 7-item flat service grid violates chunking, buries the highest-intent service**
Why it matters: "What We Do" lists 7 services with equal visual weight and no ranking. FAQ says the most common engagement is "a single high-leverage n8n workflow" — that's card #1 of 7, no different in weight from SEO Optimization. Contradicts the page's own mega-menu pattern (6 core + "View All").
Fix: cut to 3-4 featured services + "View all services →" link to services.html.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] CTA reassurance is hover-gated, invisible on mobile**
Why it matters: The only copy de-risking "Book a Call" (free, scoped live, no fluff) lives inside the desktop mega-menu's hover-only callout card. Mobile users and anyone clicking the header CTA directly never see it. Hero and footer CTAs are bare buttons with zero surrounding reassurance.
Fix: add a short reassurance line ("Free 20-min call, no pitch deck") directly under the hero and footer CTAs, not only in the hover menu.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Decorative icons not hidden from assistive tech**
Why it matters: Dozens of `material-symbols-outlined` icon spans sit next to text labels with no `aria-hidden="true"` — screen readers may double-announce icon glyph/ligature text alongside the visible label.
Fix: add `aria-hidden="true"` to all decorative icon spans.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`

**[P3] No visible custom focus states**
Why it matters: No `:focus`/`:focus-visible` styles anywhere; keyboard nav relies on browser UA defaults on a near-black background — legibility risk.
Fix: add visible focus ring using the `electric-moss` accent on nav links, CTA buttons, card links.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`
