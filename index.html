Process

From brief to live in four steps

01

Discover

We run a focused discovery call to understand your goals, current stack, and the highest-leverage problems to solve. No fluff, no lengthy proposals: just clarity.

02

Scope

We define exactly what gets built, what doesn't, and why, then give you a fixed-price quote. Scope changes are handled transparently, never used as a gotcha.

03

Build

We build in tight sprints with regular check-ins. You see working software early, not a final reveal at the end. Feedback loops are short and async-friendly.

04

Ship

We deploy, verify, and hand over full access and documentation. Post-launch, you get a clean warranty window. Then it's yours to run, no strings attached.

FAQ

Questions worth asking before you hire us

Do you work with early-stage founders or only established companies?

Both. Early-stage founders get a scoped MVP that proves the idea before you spend more. Established teams get automation and integrations layered onto what's already working, without a rebuild.

What does “production-ready” actually mean here?

Error handling, auth, logging, and a deployment pipeline, not a demo held together by happy-path clicks. If it can't survive a real user hitting it wrong, we don't consider it done.

Do we own the code and infrastructure after launch?

Yes, fully. Full source, docs, and deployment credentials are handed over at completion. No retainer required to keep it running, and no vendor lock-in on our tooling choices.

How long does a typical build take?

A single automation workflow can ship in days. A full SaaS MVP typically runs 3–6 weeks depending on scope, decided together during the Scope step, not guessed at up front.

What if I only need one thing automated, not a full product?

That's most engagements, actually. A single high-leverage n8n workflow or API integration is a complete project on its own; we don't upsell you into scope you didn't ask for.

Got something worth automating?

Tell us what's eating your team's time or what you're trying to ship: we'll scope it on the call, no discovery-deck theater.
