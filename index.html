FAQ

Questions worth asking before you hire us

Do you work with early-stage founders or only established companies?

Both. Early-stage founders get a scoped MVP that proves the idea before spending more. Established teams get automation and integrations layered onto what is already working, without a rebuild.

What does "production-ready" actually mean here?

Error handling, auth, logging, and a deployment pipeline, not a demo held together by happy-path clicks. If it cannot survive a real user hitting it wrong, we do not consider it done.

Do we own the code and infrastructure after launch?

Yes, fully. Full source, docs, and deployment credentials are handed over at completion. No retainer required to keep it running, and no vendor lock-in on our tooling choices.

How long does a typical build take?

A single automation workflow can ship in days. A full SaaS MVP typically runs 3-6 weeks depending on scope, decided together during the Scope step, not guessed at up front.

What if I only need one thing automated, not a full product?

That is most engagements, actually. A single high-leverage n8n workflow or API integration is a complete project on its own; we do not upsell you into scope you did not ask for.

Got something worth automating?

Tell us what is eating your team's time or what you are trying to ship: we will scope it on the call, no discovery-deck theater.
