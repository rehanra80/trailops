Privacy Policy

Overview

TrailOps (“we,” “us”) operates trailops.biz. This page explains what information we collect when you visit or contact us, and how we use it. We keep this simple because we only collect what we actually need to run the business.

What we collect

Contact form submissions. When you submit the form on our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, company name (if provided), and whatever you write in the message field. This is sent directly to our inbox and is not added to a mailing list or shared with third parties.

Standard server logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider logs basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps) for security and performance purposes. We don't use this for marketing or analytics profiling.

We do not currently use analytics or advertising tracking scripts, and we do not sell or rent visitor data to anyone.

Cookies

This site does not set marketing or tracking cookies. WordPress may set a small technical cookie if you interact with certain site features; none of these are used to identify or track you across other websites.

How we use your information

We use contact form submissions solely to respond to your inquiry — typically to evaluate a project, schedule a call, or answer a question. We do not use this information for unrelated marketing.

How long we keep it

We retain contact form emails as long as reasonably necessary for the business relationship or inquiry they relate to, and delete them when they're no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, request a copy of it, or ask us to delete it. Email support@trailops.biz and we'll handle it directly — no ticketing system, no runaround.

Changes to this policy

If what we collect or how we use it changes materially, we'll update this page. This policy was last reviewed on July 19, 2026.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@trailops.biz
