About TrailOps

We build the automation most agencies only talk about.

TrailOps is a small, hands-on studio for founders and teams who need working software, not slide decks. We design, build, and ship workflow automation, AI integrations, and full-stack products — then hand you the keys.

How We Started

Built by people who were tired of automation that only works in the demo

TrailOps started as one builder shipping n8n workflows and AI-integrated SaaS products for early-stage founders — and getting asked the same question on every project: “why does everyone else's automation break the moment it hits production?” So we built a studio around the opposite habit. Every workflow, chatbot, and API we ship is tested against real data, real edge cases, and real failure modes before it goes live. No proof-of-concept theater, no “we'll fix it in v2.” If it's not production-ready, it's not done.

That's still how we work today: small team, direct access to the people building your system, and a bias toward shipping something that works over presenting something that looks good in a pitch.
