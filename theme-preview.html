Direct Answers

Questions Before Engaging

Do we own the source code and infrastructure after launch?

Yes, completely. All source code, database migrations, workflow JSON definitions, and deployment credentials transfer to your accounts at final sign-off. We require zero retainers to keep your systems running.

What if I only need a single high-leverage workflow automated?

That is one of our most frequent projects. A single production-grade n8n pipeline or API integration can resolve your largest operational bottleneck in days. We never inflate scopes.

What does production-ready actually mean at TrailOps?

Production-ready means structured error handlers, automated retries, rate-limit resilience, secure token handling, and observability logging. If it breaks when an external webhook drops, it is not production-ready.
