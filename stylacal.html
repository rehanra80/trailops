Stylacal - Multi-Business Scheduling Instrument

Stylacal is an independent multi-business calendar scheduling instrument engineered to solve the structural limits of single-brand booking widgets. Designed for operators, agencies, and consultants who manage staff across independent client businesses, it guarantees strict data isolation, database-enforced double-booking prevention in PostgreSQL, 0% platform commission on client fees via direct Stripe Connect, and horizontal operational timetables.

What it Does

Full Product Capabilities Overview

Multi-Business Isolation

Operate multiple separate client businesses under one central workspace. Each retains its own booking URLs, staff permissions, client CRM, and Stripe accounts.

Cross-Business Conflict Masking

When shared staff members are assigned across brands, appointments in Brand A appear strictly as opaque, hatched "Unavailable" blocks in Brand B. Zero client notes leak.

Horizontal Staff Timetable

A calm, high-density operating timetable. Staff lanes show what is booked and what needs a decision, backed by a responsive agenda for small screens.

0% Platform Fee Stripe Connect

Stylacal takes 0% cut of customer booking payments. Direct Stripe Connect integration ensures fees deposit straight into business accounts with standard card processing.

Database Concurrency Locks

Availability and capacity limits are locked at the PostgreSQL database level using serializable isolation boundaries to eliminate race-condition double bookings.

Authenticated Client Portal

Customers manage, reschedule, and review past and future appointments via secure email OTP magic authentication without insecure link tokens or forgotten passwords.

Business-Owned SMTP Sender

Deliver transactional booking confirmations and calendar invites from your own verified business email domain (SPF/DKIM) for maximum deliverability and trust.

WhatsApp Alerts & Quiet Hours

Automated booking and reminder notifications via official Meta WhatsApp Business Accounts with strict recipient quiet hours, STOP opt-out, and TCPA compliance.
