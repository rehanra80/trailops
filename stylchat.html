Stylchat - AI Chatbot & Support Desk

Stylchat is an AI chatbot and customer support desk engineered for ecommerce and smart businesses. Styla, the AI agent, answers inquiries directly from your synced product catalogs, store pages, and knowledge base documents without requiring third-party API keys. When conversations need personal attention, your support team takes over live from the dashboard or browser extension, while chats automatically convert into tickets and synced CRM contacts. Run turnkey on managed AI, or connect your self-hosted n8n workflows (BYOI).

What it Does

Full Product Capabilities Overview

Styla AI Agent

Answers customer queries directly from your product catalog, help docs, and webpages with natural citations and zero AI API keys required.

Live Human Takeover

Alerts your team when visitors request a human. Agents take over in the same thread using the web app or Chrome and Firefox browser extensions.

Ecommerce Catalog Sync

Deep WooCommerce integration that syncs catalog items, answers product specs, adds items directly to the cart, and tracks order fulfillment live.

Support Tickets & CRM

Every chat converts into a trackable ticket with SLA tracking, agent assignment, and auto-captured customer contact cards synced to your CRM.

BYOI & n8n Webhooks

Bring Your Own Infrastructure: connect the chat widget directly to self-hosted or cloud n8n workflows for a one-time $50 lifetime license.

Visual Theme Designer

Customize colors, corner radii, avatars, fonts, light/dark themes, and custom CSS in a real-time live preview editor to match brand identity.

Native RTL Layouts

One-click right-to-left UI mirroring tailored for Arabic, Hebrew, Farsi, and Urdu across the widget header, bubbles, and composer.

Security & Domain Lock

Origin domain lock, burst rate limiting, HMAC-signed webhooks, GDPR consent gates, and country geofencing protect your budget and data.
