FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does TrailOps choose n8n over Zapier or Make?

n8n is self-hostable, meaning your operational data stays entirely under your infrastructure control without per-task subscription fees that balloon as your volume grows. n8n also supports complex branching, native JavaScript code execution, custom webhooks, and git-integrated workflow version control.

What happens if an external API (like HubSpot or Slack) goes down during a run?

Our n8n architectures include error-handling sub-workflows and dead-letter queues. If an endpoint returns a 5xx error or hits a rate limit, the execution automatically retries with exponential backoff. If it still fails, the exact payload is stored safely in PostgreSQL and an alert is sent to your tech team.

How fast can TrailOps build and deploy an n8n pipeline?

Single well-scoped pipelines (e.g. lead routing + CRM sync + Slack notification) typically ship within 3–5 days. Multi-system enterprise pipelines with approval chains and custom webhooks generally ship in 1–2 weeks with full documentation.

Will we own the workflows after the project finishes?

Yes, 100%. We export all n8n workflow JSON schemas, environment configurations, and documentation directly into your repository. You hold complete ownership with zero vendor lock-in or ongoing retainer requirements.
