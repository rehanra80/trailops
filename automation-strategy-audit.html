Audit Deliverables

Four key outputs of our Automation Strategy & Audit

1. End-to-End Operational Process Map

We interview your team leads and audit daily operations to map every manual data entry point, repeated communication step, and software disconnect across sales, support, marketing, and fulfillment.

  • ✓ Visual process flowcharts & bottleneck identification
  • ✓ Manual hours-per-week tracking per department
  • ✓ Data loss & error probability assessment

2. High-Leverage Opportunity Scoring (ROI Model)

Not everything should be automated. We evaluate every potential pipeline against an explicit ROI scoring framework (labor cost savings vs. engineering complexity), giving you a clear financial payback model.

  • ✓ Estimated monthly cost savings per workflow
  • ✓ Technical risk & API rate limit feasibility score
  • ✓ Estimated payback period (Days to ROI)

3. Sequenced Deployment Roadmap

We deliver a phased build roadmap that orders projects logically, establishing foundational database schemas first, followed by quick-win lead automations, and concluding with advanced AI inference layers.

  • ✓ Phased Sprint timelines & dependency charts
  • ✓ Fixed-price build quotes per phase
  • ✓ Complete execution plan (usable with any dev team)

4. Technical Architecture Blueprint

Honest build-vs-buy guidance. We specify the exact toolchain (e.g. self-hosted n8n vs Zapier, Supabase vs Airtable, custom Next.js vs off-the-shelf) so you avoid expensive architectural dead ends.

  • ✓ System data flow & API endpoint specs
  • ✓ Tool selection & recurring infrastructure cost estimates
  • ✓ Security, compliance & data privacy review

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an Automation Strategy & Audit take?

Our strategy engagement takes 3-5 business days. It includes a 90-minute discovery call, workflow mapping, API analysis, and a presentation of your ranked build roadmap.

What deliverables do we receive at the end of the audit?

You receive a complete visual process map, an ROI priority matrix, an API readiness scorecard, and a sequenced build plan with fixed-price quotes for implementation.

Are we obligated to hire TrailOps to build the automations?

No. The audit roadmap is yours to keep. You can implement it using internal developers, hire another team, or hire TrailOps. If you hire TrailOps, your audit cost is credited directly toward your build budget.

What size team benefits most from an automation audit?

Teams of 5 to 50 people spending 15+ hours a week on manual copy-pasting, CRM updating, or data reporting gain the highest immediate ROI from an audit.
